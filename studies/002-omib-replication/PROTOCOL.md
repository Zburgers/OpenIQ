# Study 002 — OMIB Replication

## Objective

Independently reproduce the Open Matrices Item Bank scoring/item-property workflow and evaluate OMIB as a second open measure of matrix/fluid reasoning.

## Sources

- Koch et al. (2022), DOI `10.3390/jintelligence10030041`
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9326670/
- https://osf.io/4km79/

## Key checks

- verify OSF file-level licensing/attribution before tracked redistribution;
- reproduce response scoring semantics and published 2PL parameters where source data permit;
- understand rule/property metadata and test assembly examples;
- characterize information across theta;
- document population limitations of the N=2,572 medical-school applicant calibration sample;
- compare **rank/construct behavior** with MaRs without pretending their theta metrics are already linked.

## Future linking study

A common-person and/or anchor design is required before MaRs and OMIB scores can be interpreted on a shared metric. Do not average raw theta estimates from independent calibrations.
