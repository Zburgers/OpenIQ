# D — Factor Analysis and Latent Structure

Study EFA, CFA, tetrachoric/polychoric correlations where appropriate, correlated factors, hierarchical models, bifactor models, rotation, model fit (CFI/TLI/RMSEA/SRMR with appropriate caution), cross-validation, omega hierarchical, and overfitting.

## OpenIQ question

When multidomain data arrive, does the evidence support correlated domain scores only, a hierarchical `g`, a bifactor representation, or another structure?

Do not choose the model because it produces the most marketable single number.
