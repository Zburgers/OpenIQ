# H — Fairness and Cross-Cultural Assessment

Study language/cultural loading, schooling/test familiarity, socioeconomic/access effects, translation/adaptation, accessibility, construct-irrelevant variance, sampling, stereotype/threat/context effects where evidence warrants, and fairness standards.

## India-focused questions

- Does English-medium schooling affect verbal/task-instruction performance?
- Are matrix tasks equally familiar across educational contexts?
- How should multilingual administration be adapted/validated rather than merely translated?
- Which regions/education/age groups are absent from an online convenience sample?

Fairness work must be empirical; “culture fair” is not a label we should assume for nonverbal puzzles.
