# F — Measurement Invariance and DIF

Study configural/metric/scalar invariance where applicable, multiple-group IRT, uniform/non-uniform Differential Item Functioning, DIF detection vs practical impact, anchor selection, multiple testing, and interpretation.

Potential OpenIQ grouping variables (only when justified/sample sizes permit): age, language background, education, region, sex/gender as scientifically appropriate, device/input modality, and other predeclared variables.

DIF is evidence to investigate, not an automatic accusation that an item is biased.
