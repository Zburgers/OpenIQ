# L — Ethics, Open Science, and Research Integrity

Study informed consent, participant risk, privacy, data minimization, withdrawal, minors/vulnerable groups, preregistration, selective reporting, authorship, data/code sharing, test security vs openness, FAIR/reproducible data practices, conflicts of interest, and publication ethics.

## OpenIQ rule

Do not intentionally collect a publication/generalizable-research participant dataset until the applicable ethics-review/exemption path has been established. Private self-testing/usability work does not justify later pretending that an informal convenience sample was a preregistered validation study.

## Open-science tension

Maximum openness is not always maximum validity. Public answer keys or unrestricted item exposure can damage measurement integrity. Publish methods, code, provenance, and evidence while respecting justified test-security boundaries.
