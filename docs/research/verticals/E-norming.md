# E — Norming and Score Scaling

Norming answers “relative to whom?” Study representative/reference sampling, age norms, standard scores, percentile ranks, nonlinear norming, weighting, sampling bias, norm drift, confidence intervals, and norm-update/version policy.

## Non-negotiable

A self-selected internet sample is a **reference sample**, not automatically Indian national norms or general-population norms.

## OpenIQ first step

For v0.1, report position relative to the published calibration/reference population without relabeling it as population IQ. Later define the target population before designing a norming study.
